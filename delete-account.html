Request deletion of your Easy Mandi account

Service: Easy Mandi customer website and Android app · Varanasi, India

You can request deletion of your Easy Mandi customer account and its associated personal information even if you no longer have the app installed. Requests are handled by our support team after appropriate identity verification.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the Email account deletion request button below, or send an email to champaksworld@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Easy Mandi account deletion request and provide your registered mobile number or registered email address, whichever is available. Do not send your password, OTP, payment card number or identity documents unless we later explain why a secure verification method is necessary.
  3. We will verify that you own the account, confirm the scope of the request, and process deletion. We may contact you through your registered details to prevent unauthorized deletion.

What is deleted?

Following verification, the request covers your Easy Mandi customer account, sign-in credentials and tokens, saved delivery addresses and personal information associated with your account. We will also review linked orders, delivery details and payment-related material for removal or anonymization where applicable.

What might need to be retained?

Some transaction records may need to be retained where required for tax, accounting, security, fraud prevention or handling unresolved order disputes. We will restrict their use and explain the retention categories and reasons if they apply to your request. Removing the app or merely signing out does not delete server-side records.

Need help?

If the email button does not open your mail application, email champaksworld@gmail.com directly with the subject shown above. No app login is required to access this page.
