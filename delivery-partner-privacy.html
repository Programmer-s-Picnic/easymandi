Privacy Policy — Easy Mandi Delivery Partner

App: Easy Mandi Delivery Partner (Android) · Package: live.learnwithchampak.delivery_partner · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy describes how the Easy Mandi Delivery Partner app and its associated delivery management service handle information. The app supports authorized delivery partners carrying out orders in the Easy Mandi service in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India. Developed and maintained by Champak Roy. For privacy questions and requests, email champaksworld@gmail.com.

1. Partner account information

Delivery administrators create partner accounts and may hold the partner's name, mobile phone number, account status and, where supplied through administration, a partner profile photo. Passwords are checked against stored password hashes; account/session tokens support authenticated access. The app can securely store its session token on the device so the partner can remain signed in.

2. Customer, order and delivery information

For orders assigned to a partner, the app displays relevant customer names, phone numbers, addresses, optional address map pins, order references, purchased items, delivery instructions, payment method/status and delivery stage or deadline. The backend records delivery assignments, relevant status changes, confirmation events, access/activity records and in-app notification records. Assigned order data is provided only for delivery operations and should not be used for unrelated purposes.

3. Camera and QR code scanning

With camera permission, the app can scan a customer's order handoff QR code. The scanner processes camera input to read a code and checks the order reference before sending the handoff code to the delivery server for verification. The delivery app does not intentionally record, store or upload live camera images or videos as part of this scanning feature. Camera access is not needed to sign in or read assigned orders.

4. Calling, navigation, notifications and device data

If a partner chooses Call customer, the app opens the device's phone dialer using the assigned customer's number. If the partner chooses Navigate, it opens an external maps app or service using the destination map coordinates; the external service has its own privacy practices. The delivery app does not require continuous partner GPS tracking to list or confirm deliveries. Order updates may appear as in-app notifications or device notifications according to notification settings. App version and device compatibility information may be checked for optional APK updates.

5. Why data is processed and who receives it

Information is used to authenticate delivery partners, allocate and display delivery jobs, contact customers when necessary, navigate to delivery addresses, confirm handoffs, process payment/delivery statuses, secure the service, maintain audit records and provide operational support. Authorized Easy Mandi administrators and the appropriately assigned delivery partner can access data needed for their roles. Infrastructure and hosting providers process data to operate the service; phone and maps services may receive data only when those external features are activated. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law. Delivery data is not offered for sale to advertisers.

6. Security

The app communicates with the delivery API over HTTPS. The service applies account authentication and role-based access controls, protects stored passwords using hashes, and uses access-controlled records for delivery operations. The Android app uses secure local storage for the partner session token. No connected system can promise perfect security, so partners should protect their devices and credentials and sign out when appropriate.

7. Data retention, account and deletion requests

Partner accounts, assigned order records, delivery events and notifications are retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide and administer deliveries, handle support or disputes, prevent misuse and satisfy applicable recordkeeping obligations. Retention can differ by record type. A partner may ask the Easy Mandi administrator to close their partner account and may request access to or deletion of associated personal data by writing to champaksworld@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable identity verification. Data no longer necessary will be deleted or de-identified where practicable, subject to limited legal, accounting, security or dispute-related retention requirements. We can explain any such exceptions in our response. Uninstalling the app removes local app data but does not by itself close a server-side partner account.

8. Children's privacy

The Delivery Partner app is a work tool for authorized delivery personnel and is not designed for children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when delivery operations, app functionality or legal requirements change. The revised version will appear at this public URL with an updated date.
