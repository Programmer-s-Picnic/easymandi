Privacy Policy

App: Easy Mandi (customer website and Android app) · Last updated: 10 October 2026

Easy Mandi is a grocery and fresh-produce ordering service operating in Varanasi, India. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Easy Mandi customer app and website collect, why it is used, and how you can contact us about your information. The service is developed and maintained by Champak Roy. For questions or privacy requests, contact champaksworld@gmail.com.

1. Information you provide

When you register, sign in, place an order, or contact us, we may process your name, mobile number, optional email address, encrypted/password-hashed account credentials, delivery address (including house information, locality, landmark and postal PIN), basket contents, order history, delivery instructions and communications about your orders. An email may be used for password recovery when provided.

2. Optional information and device features

With your permission, Easy Mandi can use your device location to attach a map pin to your delivery address. You can decline location access and enter the address manually. If you select UPI, you may provide a payment transaction reference and upload an image of a payment receipt for verification. The application may access the photo picker to choose that receipt; it does not need continuous access to your photographs. Local app storage may hold your cart, favorites, recently ordered items and saved account-related preferences to support the shopping experience. The website uses browser storage where necessary for account sessions and shopping.

3. Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google processes authentication and provides account identity information such as your Google account identifier, email and name as permitted by the sign-in flow. We use this information to create, verify or connect an Easy Mandi customer account. Google Sign-In is optional; our service also provides its own account sign-in.

4. How information is used

We use this information to manage sign-in and account security, display the product catalogue, save carts and addresses, process and deliver customer orders, verify payment receipts, provide order and delivery status, prevent unauthorized access, send in-app order notifications and respond to requests for assistance or data deletion.

5. Who can access or receive information

Relevant order and delivery details may be available to authorized Easy Mandi administrative staff and assigned delivery partners so that purchases can be prepared and delivered. Authentication uses Google's services if you choose Google Sign-In. The app also uses hosting/server infrastructure to operate its website, account and ordering APIs. Information may be disclosed where required by law. We do not treat your delivery information as public catalogue data.

6. Data security

The app and website communicate with backend services over HTTPS. Account session credentials are managed through access-controlled authentication, and account passwords are stored as hashes rather than readable passwords. On Android, sensitive session information is stored using secure storage. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security; please protect your login credentials.

7. Data retention and deletion

Customer account and shopping data are retained for as long as needed to operate the account, fulfill orders, handle related queries, or meet applicable security, accounting and legal requirements. When you request deletion and your identity has been reasonably verified, we will delete your account and associated personal data unless a limited record must be retained for a legitimate legal, accounting, security or dispute-resolution reason. If any records need to be retained, we will explain the relevant category and purpose when processing your request. Some information stored locally on your device can also be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Signing out alone does not delete the server account.

To request deletion of your Easy Mandi account or associated data, see Easy Mandi — Request account deletion. You can initiate the request from the app or through that page without reinstalling the app.

8. Children's privacy

Easy Mandi is a general grocery-ordering service and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us to review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page if the service, data practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be available at this URL with a revised date.
